## 4. 页面避免重复规则

| 页面类型 | 禁止写法 | 推荐写法 |
|---|---|---|
| Solutions | `Candao 提供訂單管理、配送管理、菜單管理功能。` | `當訂單、門店與配送流程分散時，團隊需要先看見問題發生在哪裡。` |
| Solutions | 大量软件截图和功能卡片。 | 大图场景、角色关系、问题选择、结果推荐。 |
| Platform | 只讲业务痛点，不展示产品如何工作。 | 每个能力必须有 UI Demo、状态变化和数据流。 |
| Platform | 把能力写成抽象价值口号。 | `新訂單進入 → 自動歸類 → 門店確認 → 狀態回傳` 这种具体流程。 |

## 5. 用于 Stitch / Figma 的页面生成提示约束

```text
Generate Solutions pages as business scenario experiences.
Do not make Solutions pages look like product feature pages.
Use real restaurant operation photography, scenario questions, role pressure, and outcome-oriented storytelling.
Only show SaaS UI in Solutions as a supporting preview or recommended capability, not as the primary page structure.

Generate Platform pages as product capability experiences.
Every Platform page must include semi-real SaaS UI, workflow steps, state changes, data flow, and a business result.
Use Capability Map, Workflow Stepper, UI Demo Panel, status drawer, dashboard, and interaction states.

Keep Solutions and Platform connected but visually different:
Solutions = problem, scenario, user role, business outcome.
Platform = capability, workflow, UI state, data flow, product proof.

Do not promote Order Management, Delivery Management, Riders App, Dispatch, Aggregated Logistics Management, Mobile Ordering, or Kiosk Ordering to first-level navigation.
```
