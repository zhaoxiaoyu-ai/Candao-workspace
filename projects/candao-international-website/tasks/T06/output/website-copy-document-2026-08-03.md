| 层级 | 建议 |
| --- | --- |
| 基础表达 | Connect orders, menus, delivery and data |
| 差异化表达 | Built for complex restaurant operations across channels, stores and markets |
| 业务场景 | Multi-store restaurant brands, multi-channel ordering, delivery orchestration, system integrations |
| 可信气质 | Practical, operational, enterprise-ready, regional experience |

T07 推荐加强的英文表达：

- Built for complex restaurant operations across channels, stores and markets.
- Manage multi-channel restaurant operations with clearer workflows.
- Connect ordering, delivery and store operations without adding complexity for teams.
- Give headquarters better visibility while helping stores stay focused on service.

T07 避免：

- 只反复使用 `connect`；
- 使用可替换到任何 SaaS 的通用表达；
- 把 Candao 写成单一订单系统、配送系统或点餐工具；
- 使用夸张形容词代替具体餐饮运营场景。
